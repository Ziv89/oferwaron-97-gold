Demo of the Live Alert notifications (signed in as an approved member). Move around the mock site below and press Send a new alert:
  • Home / Strategy, or WhatsApp Group with Live Alerts hidden → a green pop-up appears bottom-right, the alert sound plays, and on the WhatsApp Group page the bell rings and the "new" badge counts up.
  • Live Alerts open (normal or ⤢ full screen) → no pop-up: the alert appears straight in the chat with the sound, the ringing bell and a gold glow.
  • Tapping a pop-up opens the WhatsApp Group page with the chat open. Pop-ups close by themselves after 12 seconds.
